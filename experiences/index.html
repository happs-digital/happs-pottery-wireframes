What Happens in a Session

1

Arrive

Check in at the studio. Aprons and all materials are ready for you.

2

Throw

Get on the wheel. Our team guides you through the basics and stays with you the whole session.

3

Glaze

Choose a glaze from our eight signature blends. We handle the firing.

4

Collect

Pieces are ready in 2–3 weeks. We'll contact you when they're out of the kiln.

Common Questions

Do I need any experience?

No. Sessions are designed for complete beginners. Our team guides you through everything.

What should I wear?

Clothes you don't mind getting clay on. Aprons are provided but clay can still find a way.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Children must be accompanied by a participating adult. (confirm with Myles) — minimum age.

How long until I get my pieces?

Allow 2–3 weeks for drying and kiln firing. We'll contact you when they're ready to collect.

Can I post my pieces interstate?

Yes. We can ship fired pieces. Postage is charged at cost. (confirm with Myles) — shipping rates and packaging process.
