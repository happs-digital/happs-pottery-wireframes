Frequently asked questions

Where is Happs Pottery?

We're at 749 Caves Road, Anniebrook, about 10 minutes north of Dunsborough and 20 minutes from Margaret River. Look for the signs on Caves Road. There's parking on site.

When are you open?

The studio and shop are open daily from 10am to 4pm. Hours may vary during events and public holidays.

Do I need to book in advance?

For pottery experiences and studio sessions, yes. Bookings are essential as places are limited. The shop and gallery are open to walk-ins during studio hours. If you're bringing a group, get in touch early.

Can I buy pottery without doing an experience?

Yes. The Happs Pottery shop stocks wheel-thrown and hand-built ceramics: bowls, mugs, platters, and more, all in the signature glaze range. No booking needed to browse and buy.
