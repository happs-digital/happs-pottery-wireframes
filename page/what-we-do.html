The Work

Made Here, Start to Finish

Every piece in the studio is wheel-thrown by hand, glazed with one of our signature blends, and fired in the kiln on site. No outsourcing, no slip casting.

Clay is sourced from WA. Glazes are mixed in-studio, each named after a place in the South West that inspired its colour and texture.

Signature glazes
Shore Break Bunker Bay Desert Days Wyadup Stone Emerald Farmhouse Summer Days Jacaranda
