Getting Here

749 Caves Road, Anniebrook WA 6281

We're on Caves Road between Dunsborough and Yallingup — about 15 minutes north of the Dunsborough town centre. Watch for our sign on the left (heading north).

From Dunsborough

Head north on Caves Road. Studio is on the left, approximately 12km from the Dunsborough roundabout.

From Margaret River

Head south on Caves Road through Yallingup. Studio is on the right approximately 8km past Yallingup.

Parking

Free on-site parking. Accessible parking bay adjacent to the entrance.

Accessibility

The gallery and shop are step-free and accessible by wheelchair. The working studio area has some uneven flooring.

  • Accessible parking bay at entrance
  • Step-free access to gallery and shop
  • Accessible toilet on site
  • (confirm with Myles) — studio experience accessibility for wheelchair users

If you have specific access requirements, get in touch before your visit and we'll do our best to help.

Kids & Groups

Families are welcome. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult in the studio.

For school groups or larger party visits, see our Schools & Groups page or send an enquiry.

Common Questions

Can I buy directly from the studio?

Yes. Everything in the gallery is available to purchase in person. Card and cash accepted.

Do I need to book ahead?

No booking needed to visit the gallery and shop. Studio experiences do require a booking — book here.

Can I watch pottery being made?

On most days, yes. The production studio is visible from the gallery. We can't guarantee the wheel is running on every visit.
