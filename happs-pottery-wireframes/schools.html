Section 3 — What's Included
What a school session looks like
Every booking is run by an experienced pottery educator. Sessions are structured around making, not just watching — every student gets their hands in clay.
  • Introduction to the pottery wheel and hand-building techniques
  • Guided making session — each student creates their own piece
  • Educator-led demonstration by Myles or studio staff
  • Finished pieces glazed and fired — ready to collect or post
  • All materials, tools and protective aprons provided
  • Suitable for mixed ability groups — no prior experience needed
