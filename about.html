Meet Myles Happ

Myles has been throwing pottery for over 40 years from his studio on Caves Road in Dunsborough, Western Australia. His work is shaped by the South West landscape — its colours, textures, and light.

Every piece is hand-thrown, glazed with one of Myles's signature blends, and fired in the studio kiln.

Studio Values

Handmade

Every piece is thrown by hand on the wheel. No slip casting, no moulds.

Local

Clay sourced from WA. Glazes mixed in-studio. Fired on-site.

Lasting

Functional pieces made to be used daily for decades.
