Page Purpose & Outcomes

Convert school teachers, excursion coordinators, and group leaders into enquiries. Primary CTA is the group enquiry form feeding GoHighLevel. Secondary outcome: enough trust and specificity that the decision-maker can take it to a principal or committee without needing to call first.

Key Audience

Teachers and excursion coordinators researching on behalf of a group. Risk-averse — need to justify the choice to someone above them. Looking for: curriculum relevance, safety, clear pricing, easy logistics. Group size typically 15–30 students. Booking lead time weeks to months out.

Suggested Structure

  1. Hero — group/students in studio, headline + value prop
  2. Why schools choose Happs — 3 trust-building points
  3. What's included — duration, capacity, what's provided, supervision
  4. Pricing & booking — per-head or group rate, minimums
  5. Curriculum connections — arts / design / STEM / wellbeing
  6. Testimonial — teacher or coordinator quote
  7. FAQ strip — 3–4 common questions
  8. Enquiry form — school name, contact, email, phone, group size, year group, preferred dates, notes
  9. Downloadable excursion info sheet — PDF for admin approval
  10. Request a quote option — for unusual group requirements

Content Requirements

  • Confirmed pricing — per head or flat group rate (confirm with Myles)
  • Minimum / maximum group size
  • Session duration
  • What students make / take home
  • Supervision ratio or requirements
  • At least one real teacher testimonial
  • Curriculum links (creative arts, design, STEM, health & wellbeing)
  • School logistics — parking, lunch area, accessibility
  • Excursion info sheet PDF (to be created)

Tone

Warm and reassuring but professionally credible. The teacher needs to feel confident handing this to their principal. Not too casual — this is a school excursion budget decision. Lead with outcomes for students, not the studio's story.
