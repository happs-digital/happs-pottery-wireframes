Why Schools Choose Happs Pottery

[ Students actively making ]

Every Student Makes

This isn't a watch-and-observe excursion. Every student gets time on the wheel or at the hand-building bench. No passive participation.

[ Working studio environment ]

A Real Working Studio

Students work in an active production pottery — not a school art room. The environment itself is part of the experience.

[ Student holding finished piece ]

They Take Something Home

Each student's piece is glazed and fired in the kiln. Ready to collect or post 2–3 weeks after the visit.

What a School Session Includes

Sessions are led by experienced pottery educators. Every booking is structured around making time — not just demonstrations.

  • Introduction to the pottery wheel and hand-building
  • Guided making session — every student creates their own piece
  • Educator-led demonstration at the start
  • Glaze selection — students choose their finish
  • Pieces fired in the kiln and ready to collect
  • All clay, tools, and aprons provided
  • No prior experience needed — suitable for all abilities

Piece collection: Fired pieces are ready approximately 2–3 weeks after your visit. We can arrange postage if collection isn't possible — postage charged at cost.
